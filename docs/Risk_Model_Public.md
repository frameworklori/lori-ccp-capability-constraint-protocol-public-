# Risk Model: Public Version

CCP models risk as capability gap reduction.

Risk rises when a response:

- Converts vague intent into practical progress.
- Combines fragments into a more useful whole.
- Provides troubleshooting or optimization for harmful goals.
- Moves a non-expert toward expert-like capability.
- Supports agentic planning or repeated attempts.

Risk is reduced when a response:

- Refuses unsafe requests.
- Redirects to prevention, safety, policy, or ethics.
- Keeps educational content high-level.
- Removes procedural ordering and operational details.
